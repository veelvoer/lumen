# Lumen 0.10.5

- Paging and the drawer now decide like UIKit: your flick is projected to where it would coast (rate 0.998 per ms), and a flick never skips further than the neighbouring page
- Lists coast like UIScrollView: the same deceleration, so a fling glides further and settles naturally
- Crashes are now written down (Java and Rust) and shown in Settings > Performance > report, under "Last crash", to track down the crash after clearing apps
