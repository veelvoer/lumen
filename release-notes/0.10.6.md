# Lumen 0.10.6

Found by recording every frame of the transitions and spamming gestures:

- Tapping a button on a sheet (like "Got it") no longer also taps the icon underneath it
- Edit mode now animates in and out: the minus badges pop in with a spring and the +, gear and Done buttons slide and fade in
- Search always grows out of the Search capsule and folds back into it, also when opened by swiping down
- The Search capsule steps aside when the App Library slides in, so it never sits on the cards
