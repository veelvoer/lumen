# Lumen 0.2.0

A big one.

- **Drag and drop:** hold an icon, then drag it anywhere. Move between pages (hold at the edge), onto the dock, onto another icon to make a folder, or onto Remove / App info / Uninstall at the top. Drag apps straight out of the drawer onto the home screen.
- **Folders** with names (rename from the menu), opened in a popup. Drag apps out again.
- **Widgets:** at a glance, clocks (stacked, digital, analog, dot-matrix), date, battery, search. Long-press empty space > Widgets.
- **Presets rebuilt** to follow the real thing much closer: Nothing (black, red, dot-matrix clock card, single-color icons, mono labels), Pixel (Google Sans, Material You colors from your wallpaper, At a glance, search pill under the dock, suggested apps, search at the bottom of the drawer) and iOS (Inter, squircle icons, glass dock, number badges, App Library page with categories, edit mode with jiggle and remove buttons, Spotlight-style search).
- **Vector icons** everywhere in the UI, and real fonts.
- Quick menu on long press: app info, rename, hide, remove, uninstall.
- Search: apps, a calculator (type 12*7) and web search.
- Notification badges (dot or count), double tap and swipe gestures you can choose (including lock screen), Home button returns to the first page.
- Settings redesigned, with live previews of each preset. Backup and restore your whole setup through the clipboard.
