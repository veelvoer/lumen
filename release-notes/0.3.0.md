# Lumen 0.3.0

Motion and feel, plus a lot more to change.

- **New animation engine.** Everything now moves on springs that keep the speed of your finger: flick a page or the drawer and it carries on the way you threw it. Icons press in, lift, and fly into their cell when dropped. Folders open out of their own icon, menus grow out of the icon you held. Pages, settings and drawers glide in with real physics.
- **Each preset moves like the real thing:** iOS is bouncy, Pixel is smooth and decisive, Nothing is quick and mechanical. Pick your own in Settings > Animations and feel (style, speed, page effect, opening animation, blur).
- **Smoother by design:** all the slow calls to Android now run off the drawing thread, so swiping no longer stalls. Frosted blur behind the drawer, folders and App Library (Android 12+). Opening an app zooms or reveals out of its icon.
- **Settings are always reachable.** Lumen is now an app in your drawer and App Library (open it from any launcher too), long-press on empty space opens a menu with Settings on every preset, and the iOS edit mode has a gear button.
- **Change anything:** page effects, margins, dock icon size and names, name size and shadow, page indicator style, widget corner radius, folder columns, drawer order, opacity, search bar position, letter scroller, new apps on home, hide status or navigation bar, rotation, 12/24 hour clock, pinch and long-press actions.
