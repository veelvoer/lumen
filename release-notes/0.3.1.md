# Lumen 0.3.1

Tested on a real OnePlus Nord CE5 this time.

- **The wallpaper shows again.** Android's native activity asked for an opaque window, so the launcher was drawn on solid black. Lumen now asks for a translucent one.
- **Runs at the fastest refresh rate** of your screen (120 Hz on this phone) while it is in front. Swipes, flings and the drawer measured at one frame every 8.3 ms with no dropped frames.
- **New scrolling.** Your finger moves the content one to one, a fling is speed-limited and slows down steadily, and the ends give a little. Used by the drawer, search, App Library, settings and widget list.
- **No more stalls on touch:** opening the notification shade, launching apps and other calls to Android run off the drawing thread, and app icons are loaded in the background and uploaded a few per frame.
- Text on the wallpaper now follows the wallpaper (dark or light), like Pixel does, and the status bar icons stay readable on the drawer and in settings.
- Nothing preset: apps without a themed icon get a single-color silhouette of their logo instead of a gray icon.
- Pixel preset: six rows, a smaller At a glance, search bar fits its text.
- The drawer is opaque by default; drop decides by where the finger lets go.
