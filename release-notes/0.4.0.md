# Lumen 0.4.0

**Widgets from other apps.** Long-press empty space, choose Widgets, open the Your apps tab and pick any widget on your phone (weather, calendar, music, shopping...). Android asks once to allow it, then the widget lives on your home screen: tap it like normal, long-press it to make it wider, narrower, taller, shorter, or to remove it, or drag it to another place or page. Lumen's own widgets (clocks, date, battery, search) are in the Lumen tab.

Also: the system's zoom animation now plays when opening an app, the search bar rises above the keyboard, and menus on the Nothing preset are smaller. Tested on a real OnePlus Nord CE5.
