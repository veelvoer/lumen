# Lumen 0.6.0

- **Fixed the black screen after closing Lumen from the app switcher.** Lumen no longer shows up in recents, and if it is ever closed there, its process ends cleanly so the next Home press starts fresh.
- **Tablets:** rotation works (tablets always rotate), bigger grid and icons on first start, and the dock and search bar keep a sensible width on wide screens.
- **Scrolling in the app drawer works again**, and the letter scroller is now like Niagara: touch the edge and the letters bulge out around your finger with a big bubble and a tick for each letter.
- **Settings can no longer glitch** when you tap through pages quickly.
- **Resize widgets by dragging their edges:** long-press a widget, choose Resize, then drag the handles on its sides.
- **Patch notes** appear after an update (and under Settings > Updates).
- **Smoother return from apps:** no more app rescan every time you come home, and the home screen settles in with a short animation.
- **More animation:** new icons pop in, removed ones shrink away, and an icon dropped on another flies into the new folder.
- **Single-color icons keep their detail:** a logo with a button on a big shape, like YouTube, now shows the button.
