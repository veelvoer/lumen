# Lumen 0.9.0

- **Smoother swiping:** slow page swipes no longer jitter. Frames now follow your finger instead of waiting for touch events.
- **Widgets show their real look while you drag them** (before, they shrank to an icon).
- **No more glitch at the end of lists** in settings, pickers and search.
- **iOS wiggle mode** is smoother and more like iOS: eases in, every icon sways out of step.
- **iOS Liquid Glass** on the dock and the Search capsule: clear glass with a bright light edge and soft shadow rim.
- **Lumen widgets open their app:** the clock widgets open your clock, calendar widgets your calendar, battery widgets battery usage.
- **Spotlight search scrolls** (it could not scroll before).
- **App drawer:** scrolling by the letters now glides instead of jumping, and pulling down at the top closes the drawer.
- **Spam-proof launching:** tapping an app repeatedly opens it only once.
- Coming back from an app no longer fades the home screen in; it is just there.
